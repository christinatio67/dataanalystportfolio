01 / ABOUT

Engineering mindset.
Analytical perspective.

My approach to data is shaped by engineering: accuracy first, evidence over assumption, and every number traceable to its source. After eight years as an electrical engineer at PT PLN, I expanded my work into data analytics and built projects that turn raw data into practical insights. I clean and validate datasets, maintain consistency across SQL queries, spreadsheets, dashboards, and presentations, and use structured problem-solving methods such as MECE and SMART.

My work includes an analysis of 84,548 NYC property sales records and a Python propensity model developed to rank 1,000 customers for a PayLater campaign. I apply statistics and A/B testing and communicate findings clearly to technical and non-technical audiences. My interests include business intelligence, operational performance, customer analytics, and energy systems.
